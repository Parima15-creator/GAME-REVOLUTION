Welcome to ReflexRun 3D

You're running through an ancient temple with a beast on your heels. Clear obstacles to keep it behind you — the moment you stop moving, it closes in.

YOUR REST POSITION

Stand centred facing the camera, body straight, hands up near your chest. This guard stance is what every move is measured from — jump, duck, step and punch all start and end there, so return to it between obstacles. Before every run the game holds your stance, then asks for one jump, duck, dodge and punch; the fourth one starts the run for you.
